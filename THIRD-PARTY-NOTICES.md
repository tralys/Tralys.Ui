# Third-party notices

Tralys.Ui includes code and data from the projects below. Every lifted file is listed with where it came from.

## Avalonia

`src/Tralys.Ui.Engine/Fonts/Unicode/` is taken from Avalonia 11.2.2,
`src/Avalonia.Base/Media/TextFormatting/Unicode/` (https://github.com/AvaloniaUI/Avalonia), with its namespace,
visibility and whitespace changed:

- `Codepoint.cs` and `UnicodeData.cs`, cut down to the line break class and general category lookups;
- `GeneralCategory.cs` and `LineBreakClass.cs`;
- `UnicodeData.trie.cs`, generated by Avalonia's UnicodeDataGenerator from the Unicode Character Database 15.0, its
  data held in a static array.

`src/Tralys.Ui.Engine/Items/RealizedStackElements.cs` is adapted from Avalonia 11.2.2's
`src/Avalonia.Controls/Utils/RealizedStackElements.cs`, ported to `float`, its callbacks dropped or made
allocation-free.

`src/Tralys.Ui.Engine/Popups/PopupPositioner.cs` is adapted from Avalonia 11.2.3's
`src/Avalonia.Controls/Primitives/PopupPositioning/ManagedPopupPositioner.cs` (its `Calculate`, `GetAnchorPoint` and
`Gravitate`) with the edge flips of `IPopupPositioner.cs`: ported to `float`, its anchor and gravity flags merged into
one type, its screen lookup replaced by the bounds it is given, its local functions made static, its adjustment flags
dropped for one flip axis per placement with slide and resize always on, and its horizontal resize measured to the
bounds' right edge rather than their width. `IPopupPositioner.cs` takes its flag names from the xdg_shell protocol of
wayland-protocols, whose notice follows Avalonia's below.

## Prowl.Quill

The stroker in `src/Tralys.Ui.Engine/Rendering/PathTessellator.cs` (miter, bevel and round joins built from the segments'
offset edges, and flat, square, round and triangle caps) follows `Quill/PolylineMesher.cs` from Prowl.Quill
(https://github.com/ProwlEngine/Prowl.Quill), rewritten to fill reused buffers and to fade its edges over a one-pixel
fringe.

## StbImageSharp

`samples/Tralys.Ui.Demo` decodes PNG, JPEG and TGA files with StbImageSharp (https://github.com/StbSharp/StbImageSharp),
a C# port of stb_image, dual-licensed under the Unlicense or MIT; the demo takes it under the Unlicense, which places it
in the public domain. The Tralys.Ui runtime does not use it.
